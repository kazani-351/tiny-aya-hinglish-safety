# Security and content concerns

This is an AI safety research repository. Some probe scripts contain short harmful requests (no completions) used to measure and repair refusal behaviour in a language model; `README.md` lists what they are and why they are here.

To raise a concern about any content in this repository, including a request to remove something, please open an issue on this repository. Reports are read and answered there.

Model outputs to harmful prompts and the translated evaluation prompts are deliberately not published.
